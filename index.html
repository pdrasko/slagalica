How to play
25 blocks form an X shape — a center and four chains of six radiating outward. Click any unrevealed block, read the clue, and type the hidden word.

Correct → earn points and your square is marked. Wrong → turn passes to your opponent (single player: game over).

Blue-glowing blocks are connected to an already-revealed word. Each word in the chain is semantically linked to its neighbours.
