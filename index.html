How to play
Nine blocks form an X shape. Click any unrevealed block to read a clue, then type the hidden word.

A correct answer earns points and passes the turn. A wrong answer ends the game.

Points: Center word = 5 pts  ·  Arm words = 3 pts  ·  Corner words = 1 pt

Every word is linked to its X-neighbours by a shared theme.
